### Sprint 1: Foundation, Drift Detection & Experimental Models (19th – 23rd Oct 2026)
- **Sprint Goal:** Build the prediction and telemetry ingestion core, implement two-sample statistical drift detection (KS-test & PSI), train baseline models (Scikit-Learn, PyTorch Autoencoder, QML Benchmark), and configure CI.
- **Velocity Delivered:** 34 Story Points.
- **Key Deliverable Artifact:** Working MVP prediction microservice tagged [`v0.1.0-sprint1`](https://github.com/anantha-bit/self-monitoring-mlops-system/releases/tag/v0.1.0-sprint1) and 1-minute MVP video demonstration (`docs/evidence/videos/sprint1.mp4`).

- **Sprint Goal:** Implement automated recovery policies, automated validation gates (accuracy, latency, regression checks), dual-key promotion, emergency rollback, SHA-256 audit log chaining, and interactive real-time dashboard.
- **Velocity Delivered:** 34 Story Points.
- **Key Deliverable Artifact:** Comprehensive working system tagged [`v1.0.0-sprint2`](https://github.com/anantha-bit/self-monitoring-mlops-system/releases/tag/v1.0.0-sprint2) and 2-minute product video demonstration (`docs/evidence/videos/sprint2.mp4`).
